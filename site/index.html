Revit’s advanced sample project in Overhoeks, Amsterdam Noord, on the soft-soil engineering geological model under it: the most likely Robertson soil behavior type (SBTn) of the simulated cells as volumes, and the CPTs interpreted layer by layer.

Read in the browser from Parquet and Zarr on R2, among OpenStreetMap and the 3D BAG. Click an element to see what it is; further down, cross sections through the cells and the profile of a CPT.

Notebooks

  1. 01 Download model data GeoTOP voxels, CPTs and geotechnical boreholes around the building, from the BRO.
  2. 02 Simulation domains GeoTOP’s voxels grouped into simulation domains, with their CPT data compared.
  3. 03 Variograms Variograms of log₁₀ qc and log₁₀ fs fitted per simulation domain.
  4. 04 Simulation Conditional Gaussian simulation of qc, with fs co-simulated, in each domain.
  5. 05 CPT classification Each realization classified into Robertson’s soil behavior types (SBTn).
  6. 06 BimDataFrames Interpreted CPTs and SBTn volumes as BimDataFrames, published to Speckle.
